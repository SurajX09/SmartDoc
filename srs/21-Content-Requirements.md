# Content Requirements

---

# 1. Overview

This document defines the content requirements for the SmartDoc Single Page Application (SPA).

It specifies the content structure, writing guidelines, and messaging used throughout the website to ensure consistency, clarity, and professionalism.

---

# 2. Content Objectives

The website content should:

- Clearly explain SmartDoc services.
- Build trust with visitors.
- Encourage users to contact SmartDoc.
- Be simple and easy to understand.
- Be optimized for readability.

---

# 3. Tone of Voice

The content should be:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Clear
- Helpful
- Trustworthy

Avoid:

- Technical jargon
- Long paragraphs
- Confusing language

---

# 4. Hero Section Content

The Hero section shall include:

### Heading

A strong headline introducing SmartDoc.

### Subheading

A short description explaining the services offered.

### Call-to-Action

Primary CTA:

- Contact on WhatsApp

Secondary CTA:

- View Services

---

# 5. About Section Content

The About section should include:

- Business introduction.
- Mission.
- Vision.
- Commitment to customers.
- Short explanation of SmartDoc's purpose.

---

# 6. Services Section Content

Each service should include:

- Service Name
- Short Description
- Required Documents (if applicable)
- WhatsApp CTA

Descriptions should remain concise and informative.

---

# 7. Why Choose SmartDoc

This section should highlight key benefits such as:

- Trusted Assistance
- Fast Processing
- Professional Support
- Transparent Service
- Customer Satisfaction

---

# 8. How It Works

Explain the service process in simple steps.

Example:

1. Contact SmartDoc
2. Share Required Documents
3. Verification
4. Processing
5. Service Completion

---

# 9. FAQ Content

Questions should address common user concerns.

Examples:

- What documents are required?
- How long does the process take?
- How can I contact SmartDoc?
- Do you provide online support?

Answers should be short, clear, and easy to understand.

---

# 10. Contact Section

Display:

- WhatsApp Number
- Email Address
- Business Hours (Optional)

The contact information should always remain up to date.

---

# 11. Footer Content

The footer shall include:

- SmartDoc Logo
- Quick Links
- Contact Information
- Copyright Notice

Optional:

- Social Media Links

---

# 12. Content Formatting

Use:

- Short paragraphs.
- Bullet points where appropriate.
- Proper heading hierarchy.
- Clear section titles.

Avoid large blocks of text.

---

# 13. Image Content

Images should:

- Support the content.
- Be high quality.
- Be optimized for web.
- Match the SmartDoc brand.

---

# 14. Call-to-Action Guidelines

CTA buttons should use action-oriented text.

Examples:

- Contact on WhatsApp
- Get Started
- View Services
- Learn More

---

# 15. SEO Content Guidelines

Content should:

- Use relevant keywords naturally.
- Include descriptive headings.
- Include meaningful page titles.
- Include descriptive image alt text.

Avoid keyword stuffing.
