# Document Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the functional, non-functional, business, and technical requirements for the SmartDoc project.

The purpose of this document is to provide a single source of truth for the planning, design, development, testing, and deployment of the SmartDoc website. It ensures that every stakeholder involved in the project has a clear understanding of the project's objectives, scope, requirements, and expected outcomes.

This document serves as the official reference throughout the software development lifecycle and helps minimize ambiguity, improve communication, and maintain consistency during implementation.

---

# Project Summary

SmartDoc is a professional online document assistance platform designed to simplify access to various government and personal document-related services. The platform allows users to explore available services, understand the required documentation, and connect directly through WhatsApp for assistance.

The primary objective of SmartDoc is to provide a simple, trustworthy, and user-friendly digital experience for individuals seeking document-related support.
